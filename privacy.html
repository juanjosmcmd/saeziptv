Privacy policy

Sáez IPTV · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sáez IPTV is an IPTV list player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. It is free, with no ads, no purchases and no profit motive. This policy explains what data the app uses and which services your devices connect to while using it.

Summary

  • There are no user accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal data.
  • The app sends no data to the developer. There are no developer servers, no usage analytics, no advertising and no trackers.
  • Everything you set up is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores locally, without sending it to anyone:

  • The lists you add: their name, address (m3u link or server) and, where applicable, the username and password from your provider.
  • Profiles (name, color, optional PIN and whether it's a kids profile), favorites, watch history and progress, ratings and reminders.
  • Settings: app language, audio and subtitle languages, subtitle style and similar.
  • Your TMDB key, if you choose to add one.
  • If the app closes because of an error, a technical crash report that only you can see and copy if you want to send it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. The "Backup" feature creates a file only when you ask for it and wherever you choose; that file includes your list passwords, so keep it somewhere safe.

Connections to third-party services

The app has no servers of its own. While you use it, your device connects directly to these services, which receive your IP address like any internet connection:

  • Your IPTV list server, chosen by you, to download the list, the program guide and the videos. It receives your username and password for that list. The app does not control that provider or its privacy policy.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org), only if you add your own key: for posters, synopses, trends, recommendations and searches. It receives your key and the titles being looked up. TMDB privacy policy.
  • TheIntroDB and IntroDB, to know when an episode's intro starts and ends. They receive the episode's public identifiers (no data about you).
  • YouTube (Google), only when you open a trailer, which plays in the YouTube player and is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Cast, only if you use Chromecast: your device looks for Chromecasts on your network and sends them the address of the video you want to watch.

"Type it on another device" feature (QR code)

Only when you turn it on, the app opens a small server inside your Wi-Fi network for a few minutes so you can type the list or key on another device. It is not reachable from the internet, uses a random one-time key and turns off when the data arrives, when you leave that screen or after 10 minutes.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load lists and play video.
  • Notifications: only for the program reminders you set.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. Kids profiles with a PIN help families control what is watched, but content depends on the list each user adds.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated on this page with a new date.
